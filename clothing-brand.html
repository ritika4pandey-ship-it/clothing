The Founder

Mayank Kukreti

Founder · IKAVATI

IKAVATI was created with a simple vision: to build a fashion house where Indian character and contemporary design could exist beautifully together.

Inspired by India's traditions, architecture, textures and timeless clothing, Mayank Kukreti envisioned a space where every collection feels considered, personal and enduring.

For IKAVATI, luxury is not only about appearance. It is about quality, character, attention to detail and the feeling of being personally welcomed.
